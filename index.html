Leadership & Vision

Guiding Students Toward Excellence.

At Dua Academy, our vision is to help students develop the knowledge, discipline, and confidence they need to succeed in their educational journey.

Under the leadership of our principal, Sir Shahid, we aim to create a positive learning environment where students are encouraged to understand concepts, ask questions, and prepare thoughtfully for their BISE Lahore board examinations.
